SceneReady: Audition Prep

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
The actor's toolkit — sides, breakdowns, cold reads, and a scene partner.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Built by a working actor, for working actors and students. SceneReady puts your whole audition-prep workflow in one place — so the hours between "you got the sides" and "you're in the room" actually build a performance.

PREP
• Sides annotation — import scripts (PDF or text) and mark beat changes, objectives, tactics, and notes right on the page
• Character breakdown worksheets — objectives, obstacles, tactics, backstory, physicality, voice: the questions coaches actually ask, structured so you answer them

REHEARSE
• AI scene partner — run lines against a text-to-speech partner that reads the other character's dialogue, as many takes as you need, no favors owed
• Teleprompter — scroll your sides at your pace
• Cold read timer — timed sight-reading reps for the audition skill nobody practices

BETWEEN AUDITIONS
• Monologue library — keep your book warm and ready
• Rehearsal journal — what worked, what didn't, what the room said

Your scripts and notes stay on your device. No account required.

From table read to callback: walk in SceneReady.
